# Hestiat design brief

Design read: Hestiat is for travelers who want one calm place to discover stays, local experiences and useful services without the friction of a large marketplace.

Concept spine: Living Atlas. The page behaves like an atlas that opens into real places, then connects each place to the things a traveler can do there.

Delivery tier: cinema. The hero journey is the primary interaction and the surrounding interface stays editorial, useful and fast.

Animation mode: animated-website

Journey shape: single-shot. One continuous camera move keeps the story coherent and makes scroll speed control the narrative.

Journey: Discover at dawn. Enter the stay. Open toward local life. End on the place as the starting point of a trip.

World grammar: contemporary Mediterranean architecture, bone-white surfaces, deep navy shadows, cobalt details, soft daylight, realistic editorial photography, one continuous lens and locked exposure. No text or logos in the film.

Mobile framing: center-safe subject, lighter mobile encode, copy outside the focal subject.

Locked palette: bone #F4F1E8, deep navy #17263D, cobalt #2854D9, slate #6E7888, white #FFFFFF. Cobalt is the only accent and acts as the atlas navigation signal.

Locked type: Satoshi for display and interface, JetBrains Mono for utility labels.

Hero architecture: cinematic centered journey with navigation and search layered over the film, followed by an asymmetric editorial content rhythm.

Section plan: Hero journey, curated stays, experiences, services, how Hestiat works, host invitation, footer. Each section uses a different composition family.

Signature components: route-line search field, asymmetric stay rail, experience strip, split proof panel, host invitation band.

CTA inventory: hero search, stay route-link, experience text-link, host destination panel. Each intent has its own interaction garment.

Asset plan: one 15-second hero film with desktop/mobile encodes and exact-frame posters, generated Hestiat monogram, three generated stay photographs, head kit and launch cover.

Product scope: responsive Hestiat marketplace homepage with language detection, search interaction, destination/category filtering, stay cards, experiences, services, host CTA and accessible navigation. Seed data is structured to be replaced by the production marketplace database later.
